#### Decisions/Actions

**Decisions Made**

* Validation via Info Action: The committee recommended running an information action to validate DRep interest in incentive structures prior to moving forward with a full treasury withdrawal request.
* CC Incentive Model Pivot: Consensus was reached to adopt an incremental approach for CC incentives, shifting the operational focus toward direct cost reimbursement rather than direct compensation.
* Technical Endorsement Shift: The committee agreed that CIP-175 supersedes CIP-151 as the preferred technical specification for SPO governance participation, maintaining its formal endorsement for the underlying goal while updating the technical reference.
