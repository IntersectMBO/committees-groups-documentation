#### Decisions/Actions

**Decisions Made**

* Resubmission Contingency Endorsement: The Civics Committee voted (5 Yes, 2 No, 1 Abstain) to formally endorse resubmitting the Update Constitutional Committee 2026 governance action with the same four elected candidates and updated metadata if the active action fails to reach approval thresholds by September 1.
* Transition of CAP Working Group to Hiatus: The CAP working group was officially placed on hiatus until the alpha period concludes in November, transitioning to asynchronous collaboration and bi-weekly operational editor calls.
* Approval of CAP Phase 2 Specification: Following an asynchronous Discord poll reaching threshold, the CAP Phase 2 specification document was officially approved.
* Dissolution of Governance Incentives Working Group: The Governance Incentives working group was formally dissolved in its current form due to unsustainable, concentrated single-person workloads, shifting long-term incentive work directly into the broader Cardano 2030 Governance Pillar roadmap.

**Action Items**

* The Group: Complete the Intersect steering committee and board survey regarding organizational collaboration and committee remits.
* The Group: Actively engage SPOs and DReps to encourage voting on live governance actions before the September 1 deadline.
* MRI: Lead targeted outreach to non-voting stake pool operators and community groups to gather qualitative feedback on why voting thresholds remain low.
* IH: Ensure upcoming Intersect upgrade bulletins, weekly update blogs, and daily X posts explicitly detail the hard fork consequences of non-participation.
* IH: Publish communications clarifying that SPOs can submit votes for both parameter changes and committee updates in a single transaction.
* BA: Coordinate with Carlos Lopez de Lara and developer ecosystem figures to produce short video content driving voter participation.
* TL: Update the Intersect Knowledge Base with the approved CAP Phase 2 document.
* The Group: Review NC's shared Cardano 2030 Governance Pillar document to prepare for a deep dive session during the next meeting.
