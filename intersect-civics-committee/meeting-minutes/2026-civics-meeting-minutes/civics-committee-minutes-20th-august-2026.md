#### Decisions/Actions

**Decisions Made**

* Civics Ownership of Governance Pillar 3: The committee agreed to position itself as the strategic coordinator and roadmap owner for Pillar 3 (Governance) of Cardano Vision 2030, leaving technical execution to decentralized vendors.
* Pausing New Working Group Creation: To minimize bureaucratic overhead and prevent resource fragmentation, the committee decided against creating a dedicated working group for the 2030 roadmap.
* Narrow Technical Scope for Dijkstra Amendment: The committee recommended keeping the upcoming Dijkstra constitutional amendment strictly technical—limited to required Leios protocol parameters—to avoid conflation with broader changes.
* Joint Social Media Outreach Spaces: The committee aligned with Intersect to host regular Twitter/X Spaces on Tuesdays (August 25 and September 1) to drive SPO mobilization for the active CC vote.
