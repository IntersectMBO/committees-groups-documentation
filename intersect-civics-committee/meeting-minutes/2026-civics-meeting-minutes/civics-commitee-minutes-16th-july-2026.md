#### Decisions/Actions

**Decisions Made**

* Unanimous Endorsement of CIP-151: The committee unanimously voted to formally endorse CIP-151 regarding on-chain stake pool registration keys, aiming to secure its inclusion in the scope of the next protocol hard fork.
* Resumption of X Spaces: Consensus was achieved to officially resume monthly Civics Committee X Spaces starting the following week, supported by a structured 12-week host and co-host roster.
* Shift toward Holistic CC Incentives: The committee evaluated Danielle's proposal regarding CC member legal support and determined that future models must prioritize comprehensive, forward-looking compensation rather than isolated, retroactive risk reimbursement.
