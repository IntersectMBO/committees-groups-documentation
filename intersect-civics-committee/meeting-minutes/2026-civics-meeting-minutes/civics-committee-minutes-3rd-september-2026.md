#### Decisions/Actions

**Decisions Made**

* CC Governance Action Enacted: The Update Constitutional Committee governance action was officially approved on-chain and enacted, seating new members Marek Mahut and Leandros BSP alongside reconfirmed members.
* CAP Bug Transparency Protocol: The committee agreed to track alpha bug fixes internally on GitHub and compile a comprehensive close-out report at the end of the alpha phase rather than publishing frequent individual bug posts.
* Adopt Swim Lane Framework for Vision 2030: The committee formally agreed to structure its execution of the Cardano Vision 2030 governance goals using a swim lane roadmap format with distributed member champions.
* Asynchronous Transition for CAP Working Group: The CAP working group officially transitioned to an asynchronous operational model, utilizing bi-weekly editor calls rather than weekly general meetings.
