| A phased approach to Attendance & Accountability | <p>- The group debated the existing 8-week timeline for dismissal, with general consensus that 8 weeks of total inactivity is too long under a 2-year term.</p><p>- Simo emphasized the need to differentiate between bad behavior (ghosting) and excused absences (notifying the secretary/chair due to health or work).- Lorenzo suggested a phased system instead of jumping straight to extreme dismissal: Inactivity → Lose Reward → Lose Voting Power → Formal Dismissal Case.</p> | Working Group: Outline a step-by-step accountability framework on the Miro board, defining how members lose and gradually earn back their rights through consistent behavior.                         |
| Quantifying Committee Member Contributions       | <p>- The group analyzed how to measure performance based on the newly approved Version 1 baseline.</p><p>- Gintama suggested tracking participation in formal asynchronous votes where rationales are required.</p><p>- Thiago noted that if a member misses a live meeting but actively anchors localized execution (like running regional workshops), their structural contribution should still be deemed fulfilled.</p>                                                              | Lorenzo: Use these scenarios to draft the parameters for the Version 2 guidance documentation.                                                                                                        |
| Standardizing Meeting Cadences & Mandatory Calls | <p>- Thiago proposed that chairs should have the power to mandate attendance for high-priority alignment meetings (e.g., ahead of a critical vote), with a baseline warning system if missed.</p><p>- The team noted that enforcing rigid cross-committee rules is difficult because cadences vary wildly: the Budget committee meets twice a week, while the MCC meets bi-weekly.</p>                                                                                                   | Working Group: Keep discussing whether minimum thresholds can be standardized or if authority should be delegated to individual committee charters.                                                   |
| Rewards Disputes & Value Pegging                 | <p>- Thiago highlighted a potential loophole where inactive members could falsify their text-based monthly contribution reports just to claim starter rewards.</p><p>- Thiago also echoed board suggestions to peg governance stipends to stable dollar values rather than raw ADA to insulate volunteers from market volatility.</p>                                                                                                                                                    | <p>Thiago: Prepare to bring the currency-pegging topic back to the ISC for broader strategic review</p><p>.Lorenzo: Look into a structural "challenge window" between reward claiming and payout.</p> |
