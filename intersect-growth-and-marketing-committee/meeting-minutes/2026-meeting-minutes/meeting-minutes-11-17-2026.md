## Decisions/Actions

**Decisions**

* **Focus Areas for 2027 Objectives:** The committee confirmed that Growth and Marketing will focus its review and feedback on Pillar 2 (Adoption and Utility) and Pillar 4 (Community and Ecosystem Growth) of the 2030 framework.
* **Reclassification of Marketing Objectives:** Members agreed to move the first four demand-generation and narrative-support objectives out of Pillar 2 and into Pillar 4 so they align under the marketing domain rather than adoption.
* **Feedback Timeline:** The committee agreed to review the 2027 objectives document and submit initial comments by Friday evening, followed by an internal team sync the following Tuesday.
* **Absentee Member Escalation:** The committee established a deadline of Monday, November 24, for absent members Reshan and Maggie to respond to outreach emails regarding their attendance, with the option to bring the matter forward for an official discussion on Tuesday.
* **AI and Government Vertical Staffing:** Laura and Marco decided to collaborate on the AI and government vertical (alongside the capability map) by bringing in an external subject matter expert (SME) from Laura's team to assist Marco.&#x20;
