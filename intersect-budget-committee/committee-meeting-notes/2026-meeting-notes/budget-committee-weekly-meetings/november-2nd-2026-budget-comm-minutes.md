## Discussion/Actions

**Quorum:** Confirmed

### 1. Opening and Meeting Time

Secretaryopened the meeting and confirmed quorum.

The Chair raised concerns about the current meeting time following daylight-saving changes, as the call now starts very early for some members.

The committee discussed several alternative time slots and days, including moving the meeting to Wednesday. Because of conflicts with other Intersect and ecosystem meetings, no new time was agreed during the call.

It was agreed to collect members’ availability asynchronously and identify a new meeting slot that works across time zones.

### 2. 2027 Budget Process Proposal

The committee discussed the recently published proposal for the 2027 Budget Process.

Members were encouraged to review the blog and provide feedback through the available public discussion channels, either individually or as part of the Budget Committee.

It was noted that Intersect and Cardano Foundation are collecting community feedback and preparing additional supporting material, including responses to frequently raised questions.

The current publication represents a high-level framework rather than a fully defined process. More detailed work is expected if there is sufficient community and DRep support for the proposed direction.

### 3. Role and Authority of Domain Stewards

A significant part of the discussion focused on the proposed role of domain stewards.

Members generally supported the concept of using subject matter expertise to help structure and assess funding within specific domains, but raised concerns that the current framework does not yet provide enough detail on the authority, responsibilities, and limitations of stewards.

Questions included:

* How much influence stewards would have over proposal requirements.
* Whether they could determine what information proposers must provide.
* How much influence they could have over which proposals receive attention from DReps.
* How conflicts of interest would be managed.
* How the objectivity and independence of stewards would be protected.
* What safeguards would prevent individual opinions from becoming de facto gatekeeping.

It was emphasized that these issues will need to be addressed in the detailed design of each domain and stewardship process.

### 4. Stewardship, Gatekeeping and DRep Authority

Members discussed the importance of ensuring that stewards do not become gatekeepers to treasury funding.

It was clarified that, from Intersect’s perspective, stewardship should facilitate the process and provide expertise rather than decide which proposals are allowed to proceed.

Ultimate decision-making authority would remain with DReps.

The proposed framework is based largely on social agreement and governance approval rather than technical on-chain enforcement. As a result, proposals could technically still be submitted outside the agreed process.

Members acknowledged that this makes community and DRep commitment to the framework particularly important.

### 5. Separation of Responsibilities

Concerns were raised about potentially concentrating too many responsibilities within a single steward.

Members discussed whether the same steward should be involved in:

* Helping define the domain budget.
* Designing the proposal process.
* Evaluating or assessing proposals.
* Monitoring delivery and performance.

It was suggested that clearer separation of responsibilities may be required to provide appropriate checks and balances.

Members agreed that this should be explored further when more detailed stewardship processes are developed.

### 6. Conflict of Interest and Steward Independence

The committee discussed potential conflicts of interest, particularly in highly specialized areas where many of the available experts are also active ecosystem participants, vendors, or contributors.

Members noted that strong safeguards will be necessary to maintain trust in the process.

Potential measures discussed included:

* Clear conflict-of-interest rules.
* Public codes of conduct.
* Standardized and transparent assessment processes.
* Data- and evidence-based evaluations.
* Clear mechanisms for reporting inappropriate pressure or influence.
* Ensuring decisions reflect an institutional process rather than the views of a single individual.

The importance of transparency was repeatedly emphasized as a key mechanism for protecting both stewards and the wider community.

### 7. Transparency and Standardization

Members discussed the value of establishing common standards for how stewards operate and report.

A standardized approach could help ensure that proposal assessments, reporting, and decision-making are consistent across domains and are not dependent on individual preferences.

It was suggested that the detailed stewardship model should include clear expectations for:

* Reporting.
* Transparency.
* Evidence used in assessments.
* Governance and oversight.
* Conduct and conflicts of interest.
* How recommendations are communicated to DReps.

Members noted that these standards could help address concerns from vendors and community members about how stewardship would work in practice.

### 8. Community Feedback and Next Stage of the 2027 Process

The committee emphasized that the current proposal is intended to test whether the overall framework has sufficient community support before significant effort is invested in detailed process design.

If the approach receives support, the next stage will require detailed work on each domain, including stewardship models, funding allocation, governance processes, and reporting standards.

The Budget Committee is expected to contribute to that work, alongside other committees, DReps, stakeholders, and the wider community.

If the proposed framework does not receive sufficient support, the process may need to be revised or reconsidered.

### 9. Actions and Next Steps

* Collect members’ availability and agree a revised Budget Committee meeting time.
* Committee members to review the published 2027 Budget Process proposal and provide feedback.
* Continue collecting community and DRep feedback on the proposed framework.
