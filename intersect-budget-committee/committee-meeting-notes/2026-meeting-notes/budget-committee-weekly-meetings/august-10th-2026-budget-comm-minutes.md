### 2. Ecosystem Funding Working Group Presentation

A representative from the Cardano Foundation presented an initiative led through the Ecosystem Funding Working Group under the Product Committee, aimed at developing a more coordinated and strategic approach to ecosystem funding.

The initiative is funded by the Cardano Foundation as a public good and explores how different funding mechanisms can better align with Cardano 2030 objectives.

The project includes an assessment of Cardano's existing funding landscape, stakeholder interviews, and the development of an initial ecosystem funding framework.

The proposed framework focuses on four key areas:

* **Domain-based allocation:** Structuring funding around strategic priorities and funding categories.
* **Builder accessibility:** Improving visibility and connections between builders and funding opportunities.
* **Return and recoverability:** Considering the financial sustainability and effectiveness of treasury funding.
* **Layered KPIs:** Establishing measurable outcomes at ecosystem, funding domain, and project levels.

The committee discussed the importance of measuring actual outcomes rather than only project deliverables, establishing accountability for monitoring results, and incorporating perspectives from successful builders and external industry experts.

Members recognized that the research could provide valuable input into recommendations for the 2027 Net Change Limit (NCL) and improvements to the 2027 Budget Process.

The committee was invited to review the published research and provide feedback through the consultation form by **August 21, 2026**. The final research deliverables are expected by the end of August, with further community engagement and collaboration to be discussed.

### 3. Budget Process Retrospective and Stakeholder Outreach

The committee reviewed progress on expanding stakeholder engagement following the previous meeting's discussion about the limited number of retrospective survey responses.

A shared outreach tracking spreadsheet was presented, containing a list of DReps and space for committee members to identify stakeholders they can contact.

Members agreed that feedback should not be limited to formal survey responses. Direct conversations, emails, and individual calls could provide more meaningful insights, particularly from DReps and other stakeholders who may not complete the survey.

The committee discussed developing a short set of questions to guide these conversations, focusing on:

* **Process timing:** When the 2027 Budget Process should begin and whether a more continuous or quarterly approach would be beneficial.
* **Funding categories:** Whether treasury funding should be structured into thematic categories or funding buckets.
* **Process improvements:** What should be started, stopped, or continued based on the 2026 experience.

Members also discussed approaching organizations and proposal submitters that did not participate in the 2026 Budget Process to understand their reasons and identify improvements that could encourage participation in future cycles.

Timing was highlighted as a significant concern, particularly where funding requirements do not align with the budget process schedule.

The committee agreed that a common outreach approach and a concise set of questions should be developed before members begin contacting stakeholders. The possibility of establishing measurable outreach targets was also raised.

### 4. Planning for the 2027 Budget Process

The committee briefly discussed how retrospective feedback and the Ecosystem Funding Working Group's research could inform preparations for the next budget cycle.

Members emphasized the importance of determining when the 2027 Budget Process should begin, noting that stakeholder feedback on timing could provide valuable evidence for discussions with the Board and Intersect.

The committee also recognized the need to move beyond collecting feedback and begin considering how the findings could translate into practical improvements.

A more detailed discussion was deferred to a future meeting when additional committee members could participate.

### 5. Product Committee KPI Survey

The committee briefly discussed the Product Committee's request for feedback on the Cardano 2030 KPIs.

It was clarified that the Budget Committee is expected to provide one consolidated response representing the committee's collective views, rather than individual submissions from each member.

The committee agreed to review the survey questions in advance and discuss its feedback at the next meeting.
