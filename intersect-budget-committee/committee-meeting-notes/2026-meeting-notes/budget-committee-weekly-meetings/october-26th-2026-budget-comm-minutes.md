## Discussion/Actions

**Quorum:** No

### 1. Opening

Simo Simovic opened the meeting and introduced the main topic: review of a draft questionnaire prepared to help the committee decide whether to proceed with the proposed Treasury Administration Working Group and, if so, what its scope and priorities should be.

The questionnaire was intended to capture committee members' views on the proposed work, their willingness and capacity to contribute, and whether other priorities should take precedence.

### 2. Review of the Working Group Questionnaire

The committee discussed whether the questionnaire was the right next step before formally creating a working group.

Members generally supported first understanding whether there is sufficient interest and capacity to carry out the work.

A key question identified was whether committee members are realistically able and willing to contribute time to the initiative. It was noted that if very few members are available, this may determine whether the working group is feasible regardless of the other questionnaire responses.

The committee also discussed converting the questionnaire into a Google Form after the questions have been reviewed and finalized.

### 3. Working Group Capacity and Participation

Members discussed the practical challenges of running the proposed working group.

Some members noted that previous committee working groups had struggled because participants did not have enough time to contribute consistently.

It was clarified that an Intersect working group could include people outside the Budget Committee, allowing interested Intersect members and other contributors to participate alongside a smaller number of Budget Committee members.

This could increase capacity while allowing the Budget Committee to review and approve the outputs.

Members also clarified that the proposed work is primarily intended to review administration, transparency, and delivery assurance processes rather than independently reassess every funded project's individual milestones and deliverables.

### 4. Need for Clear Impact and Purpose

Several members questioned whether the proposed initiative would create sufficient value relative to the time and effort required.

Concerns were raised that the committee risked creating additional process and bureaucracy without first being clear on the expected impact.

Members agreed that the purpose, expected outcomes, and scope of any working group need to be clearly defined.

The committee discussed potentially narrowing the work so that it focuses on a smaller number of improvements with clear value rather than attempting to review every aspect of treasury administration and transparency.

### 5. Intersect Support and Implementation

The committee revisited the question of whether recommendations developed by the working group would ultimately be considered or implemented by Intersect.

Members emphasized that it would not be worthwhile to invest significant time in the initiative if the relevant Intersect teams were not interested in the work or willing to consider the recommendations.

Simo Simovic agreed to discuss the proposed initiative internally and clarify whether there is support for the work.

### 6. 2027 Budget Process Update

An update was provided on the proposed framework for the 2027 Budget Process.

The initial framework was expected to be published for wider community review, with the intention of collecting feedback from DReps and other ecosystem stakeholders over the following weeks.

The proposed approach is expected to introduce a more structured budget model, including funding domains and clearer processes for allocating treasury funding.

Members noted that publication of the framework should provide greater clarity on the Budget Committee's role and create more concrete work for the committee around the detailed design of the 2027 process.

It was emphasized that the framework is still a proposal and will require community support and further refinement before being finalized.

### 7. Budget Committee Involvement in the 2027 Process

The committee discussed the fact that voting members had not been directly involved in developing the initial 2027 framework.

This led to a broader discussion about committee roles, information sharing, and how the Budget Committee should participate in future process design.

Members noted that the Budget Committee had played a significant role in developing previous budget processes and should have a meaningful role in reviewing and developing the detailed mechanics of the 2027 process.

It was also acknowledged that the wider Intersect committee structure still has gaps around responsibilities, escalation, information sharing, and how committees interact with the Board and administration.

Once the proposed framework is public, the Budget Committee is expected to review it, provide feedback, and contribute to the detailed process design if the overall direction receives sufficient support.

### 8. Community Socialization and DRep Engagement

The committee discussed the importance of socializing the proposed 2027 framework with DReps and the wider community.

Members emphasized that the new approach represents a significant change from previous budget processes and will require broad understanding and support.

The process is expected to include gathering feedback from DReps and other stakeholders and incorporating that feedback before the framework is finalized.

If sufficient support cannot be established, the existing budget process remains available as a fallback approach, potentially with selected improvements.

### 9. Meeting Schedule

The committee briefly discussed the impact of daylight-saving time changes on the weekly meeting schedule.

The meeting remains fixed in UTC, meaning the local time changes for members in regions that observe daylight-saving adjustments.

The committee may revisit the meeting time or day if attendance becomes difficult.

### 10. Actions and Next Steps

* Committee members to review the draft Treasury Administration Working Group questionnaire and provide comments or suggested changes.
* Finalize the questionnaire and convert it into a form for voting members to complete.
* Use the responses to assess whether there is sufficient interest, capacity, and alignment to proceed with the working group.
* Review the proposed 2027 Budget Process framework once published.
* Collect Budget Committee feedback on the proposed 2027 approach and contribute to further process design.
* Revisit the Budget Committee's role and responsibilities as the 2027 process develops.
