## Discussion/Actions

**Quorum:** Confirmed

### 1. Opening and Meeting Schedule

The meeting opened with a follow-up discussion on finding a better meeting time after daylight-saving changes.

The availability exercise showed that there was no single time where all voting members were fully available. Members discussed whether to move the call to a different day and also considered reducing the meeting cadence.

Given the committee’s current workload and uncertainty around its role in the 2027 Budget Process, a bi-weekly meeting cadence was suggested as a possible temporary option.

Members agreed to update their availability and continue trying to identify a workable slot, with Wednesday emerging as one potential option.

### 2. Year-End Planning

The committee discussed the remaining work before the end of the year.

The Annual Member Meeting is scheduled for the week of December 7, and the committee will need to prepare a summary of:

* Q4 achievements.
* Overall 2026 achievements.
* Priorities and direction for 2027.

It was noted that Intersect expects a reduced operating period during the final two weeks of December, so the committee has only a few remaining working weeks to complete its year-end review and initial 2027 planning.

### 3. Proposed Intersect Committee Restructure

An update was provided on discussions taking place within the Intersect Steering Committee regarding the future committee structure.

The current recommendation under discussion would consolidate the existing structure into four broader committees:

* MBO Steering Committee.
* Cardano Technical Committee.
* Cardano Governance Committee.
* Cardano Adoption Committee.

The recommendation has not yet been finalized or approved and would ultimately require a Board decision.

The proposed structure was developed by starting from Intersect’s expected areas of work and then considering which committee structure would best support those responsibilities.

### 4. Potential Future of the Budget Committee

Under the current restructuring proposal, the Budget Committee would no longer necessarily continue as a standalone committee.

One option under discussion is for it to continue as a council or specialist group under the MBO Steering Committee, focused specifically on the Cardano Budget Process.

Another possibility discussed was whether the budget process fits more naturally under the broader Cardano Governance area.

No decision has been made, and the definition of what a “council” would mean in practice is still being developed.

Members were assured that, if the structure changes, existing Budget Committee members would be consulted about the new model and whether they wish to continue participating.

### 5. Intersect’s Role in the Cardano Budget Process

The committee discussed whether the restructuring proposal means Intersect intends to step away from facilitating the Cardano Budget Process.

It was clarified that this is not the current expectation.

While the latest Intersect budget proposal does not explicitly commit the organization to running the Cardano Budget Process, relevant funding could still be used to support critical ecosystem processes.

The Budget Process was described as one of those processes where Intersect can continue to provide facilitation and coordination, even though the Cardano Constitution does not require a specific organization to operate it.

Members acknowledged that the process is ultimately open and decentralized, but that facilitation has practical value.

### 6. Committee Effectiveness and Current Role

The discussion returned to the question of how useful and effective the Budget Committee has been in recent months.

Members acknowledged that the committee has had less concrete work since completion of the previous budget process and that its current role and authority are unclear.

This uncertainty is linked both to the ongoing development of the 2027 Budget Process and the broader review of Intersect’s committee structure.

Members agreed that the remaining weeks of 2026 should be used to reflect on what the committee has delivered, what role it should play going forward, and where it can provide the most value.

### 7. Transition and Timing

If the proposed committee restructure is approved, further work will be needed to determine how and when the transition should take place.

Potential timing discussed included implementation from the beginning of 2027 or later in the year, potentially closer to the April committee election cycle.

It was emphasized that the transition needs to be carefully planned because a large number of members and existing committee responsibilities would be affected.

More information is expected once the Intersect Steering Committee finalizes its recommendation and the Board considers the proposal.

### 8. Actions and Next Steps

* Members to update their meeting availability so a revised meeting slot can be identified. Check whether the proposed Wednesday slot is workable for all members.
* Consider a temporary bi-weekly meeting cadence while the committee has limited active work.
* Prepare a draft summary of Q4 and overall 2026 Budget Committee achievements.
* Begin developing the committee’s proposed priorities and direction for 2027.
* Share the committee restructuring draft with Budget Committee members for review.
* Continue discussion on the Budget Committee’s future role once the ISC and Board provide further direction.
* Prepare year-end materials for the Annual Member Meeting in the week of December 7.
