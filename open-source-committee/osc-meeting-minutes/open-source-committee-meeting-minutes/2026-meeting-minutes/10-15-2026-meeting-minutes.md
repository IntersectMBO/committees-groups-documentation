## Decisions/Actions

**Decisions**

* **Approval of MRP Excess Fund Reallocation:** Approved the reallocation of excess Maintainer Retainer Program funds (approximately 700,000 ADA) to cover deficits in other OSC budget programs, specifically prioritizing the Bug Bounty Program (passed with 4 votes in favor, 2 abstentions).
* **Maintainer Retainer Program Cohort Scope:** Agreed that no additional maintainers will be onboarded to the Maintainer Retainer Program for the remainder of the program cycle.
* **Tooling Sustainability Program Closeout and Treasury Sweep:** Approved the wind-down of the Tooling Sustainability Program by the end of 2026, agreeing not to draw down the remaining \~1.1 to 1.2 million ADA allocation and deciding to sweep any unused funds left in the wallet back to the Treasury reserve smart contract ahead of the December break.
* **Decline Funding for KardanoSDK:** Determined not to move Javier’s Cardano SDK project forward to the next step for tooling funding, advising the creator to establish organic community adoption and coordinate with existing ecosystem tools first.
