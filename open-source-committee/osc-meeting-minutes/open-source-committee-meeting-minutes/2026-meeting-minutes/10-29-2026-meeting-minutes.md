## Decisions/Actions

**Decisions**

* **Reallocate Onboarding Milestone Funds to Bug Bounty:** Agreed to submit and claim the upcoming maintainer onboarding milestones (Milestones 3 and 5, valued at 300,000 and 100,000 ADA respectively) and immediately convert/reallocate those funds to the bug bounty program.
* **Proceed with "First Five Funded" Tooling Report:** Decided to finalize and ship the milestone report detailing the four active tools (Cicero, Plutonomicon, CTL, and Lambda Buffers) while explicitly documenting the unresolved blockers regarding Mesh AI's statement of work, rather than delaying the submission to add or replace projects.
* **Cancel Remaining Unclaimed Tooling Milestones:** Decided to pull the single 500,000 ADA milestone associated with the first tooling report and cancel the remaining downstream milestones under this allocation.
* **2025 Treasury Withdrawal Cutoff and Fund Sweeping:** Agreed not to claim any additional milestones beyond the end of 2026 under the current treasury withdrawal; all claimed funds will be held in the OSC wallet earmarked for specific active contracts, and any unallocated funds will be swept back to the treasury.
* **Close Out Developer Advocate Cohort 2 as Delivered:** Decided to mark the outstanding tasks from the developer advocate cohort as closed/done, accepting the deliverables as-is without attempting to retroactively withhold compensation, and to document procedural shortcomings as lessons learned for future cohorts.
* **Maintain Discord as the Primary Channel for Member Tasking:** Decided against instituting a weekly task-assignment email system for committee members, keeping Discord as the primary platform for distributing tasks, sharing documentation, and tracking responsibilities.
* **Reimburse Unused Expansion Milestone Funds to Intersect:** Confirmed previous committee poll decisions to formalize the return/reimbursement of funds earmarked for the expansion milestones of the Maintainer and Sustainability programs in order to focus strictly on existing projects.
