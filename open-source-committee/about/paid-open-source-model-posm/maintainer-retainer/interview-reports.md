# Interview Reports

Round 1 Interviews: 8 of 15 Applicants selected for various 'Core' and Community Projects.

#### Open Source Committee Interview Report Summary

Following a careful review of the 15 applicants in the first round of the Maintainer Retainer Program (MRP), the Open Source Committee (OSC) selected 8 individuals for onboarding. This selection process included separate interviews with project teams (when required) to ensure strong alignment and cohesion. Overall, the committee evaluated an exceptionally strong cohort of candidates, characterized by deep technical expertise within the Cardano ecosystem, and a consistent commitment to collaborative, professional open-source contribution.

Round 2 Interviews: 8 of 11 Applicants selected for various 'Core' and Community Projects.

#### Open Source Committee Interview Report Summary

Following a careful review of the 11 applicants in the second round of the Maintainer Retainer Program (MRP), the Open Source Committee (OSC) selected all 11 individuals for onboarding. During the contract phase, 3 Maintainers chose to step away from the program for personal reasons. This selection process included separate interviews with project teams (when required) to ensure strong alignment and cohesion. Overall, the committee evaluated an exceptionally strong cohort of candidates, characterized by deep technical expertise within the Cardano ecosystem, and a consistent commitment to collaborative, professional open-source contribution.

Round 3 Interviews: 7 of 10 Applicants selected for various 'Core' and Community Projects.

#### Open Source Committee Interview Report Summary

Following a careful review of the 10 applicants in the third round of the Maintainer Retainer Program (MRP), the Open Source Committee (OSC) selected 7 individuals for onboarding. The 3 Maintainers that were not selected, were unable to adequately answer interview questions, additionally it was difficult to prove ties to and experience within the Cardano ecosystem. This selection process included separate interviews with project teams (when required) to ensure strong alignment and cohesion. The final round of interviews featured a cohort of candidates that presented unique technical expertise inside and outside of the Cardano ecosystem. The selections reflect a focused approach to support desirable community projects and fulfillment of a mission to expand the reach of the Maintainer Retainer Program.<br>
