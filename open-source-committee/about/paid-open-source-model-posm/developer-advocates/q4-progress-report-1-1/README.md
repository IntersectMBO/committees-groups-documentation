## Summary

This report documents Q2 2026 deliverables progress demonstrated by the four Developer Advocates, as defined in their [Consultant Services Agreements](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Te0JAe4pGf6VsOaRIyGTSv-ks8iZEa6F/edit). Deliverables executed during the quarter align with individual and collaborative goals covering developer experience, onboarding, documentation, community engagement, strategic collaboration, and ecosystem growth. 50+ deliverables were tracked, including individual contributions from each Developer Advocate and collaborative milestones.

Details regarding milestone progress and deliverables can be found on the publicly available GitHub dashboard [GitHub.com/IntersectMBO/OpenSourceOffice](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/Open-Source-Office) under the[ Developer Advocate Program (Project 71)](https://github.com/orgs/IntersectMBO/projects/71). Technical contributions, documentation, working group resources, and supporting materials can be found in the[ Developer Experience repository](https://github.com/IntersectMBO/developer-experience) and on the[ DevEx website](https://devex.intersectmbo.org/?utm_source=chatgpt.com).&#x20;



**Key Outcomes**

* **Developer Experience Repository:** All advocates met the minimum PR requirement, with more than 20 PRs merged across the cohort. The team published the Q2 DevEx changelog and logged developer feedback on repository usability.
* **Deepen Strategic Collaborations:** The advocates reached out to a range of ecosystem projects, community hubs and outside organizations. This work produced a jointly reviewed SDK integration guide, a proposed partnership to bring more women developers into the ecosystem, a project spotlight, and new introductions between Cardano projects and potential partners.
* **Diversity & Inclusion Programs:** The advocates held several regional and language-specific workshops, both in person and online, for audiences in Europe, Africa and Francophone communities. They also published or drafted onboarding guides for new and underrepresented contributors.
* **Expand Ecosystem with New Projects:** The advocates published step-by-step build guides for newcomers. The guides cover frontend and wallet integration, core protocol contribution, off-chain SDK development and local emulator setup, and one was merged into the Cardano Developer Portal.
* **Foster Innovation & Technology Advancement:** Each advocate ran a technical demo and held a feedback session, including sessions with core contributors. They documented developer pain points with proposed solutions and combined them into a shared Q2 DevEx Report.
* **Build a Global Community:** The advocates organized community events across several regions, both in person and online. They also published educational resources, including a translation into a local language.
