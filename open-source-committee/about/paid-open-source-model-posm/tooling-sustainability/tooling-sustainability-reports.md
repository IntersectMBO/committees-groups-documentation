# Tooling Sustainability Reports

**Report 1: First 5 Funded**

The Open Source Committee (OSC) has dedicated significant time to ensure that proper review is taking place with regards to funding various Cardano tools. The First 5 Funded Report documents progress on the first five tools funded under the program, covering milestone delivery, adoption evidence, and compliance status against the program's funding rules. To date of publication, 6 milestones have tracked across the five funded tools, totaling \~431,730ADA / $95,250 disbursed against completed deliverables.
